# Pharmacy Inventory Analysis

## Key Findings

### 1. Stockout Risk
- Insulin and Ventolin show high demand compared to stock
- Risk of shortage due to long lead time

### 2. Overstock Risk
- Paracetamol stock is higher than demand
- Risk of capital lock and wastage

### 3. Expiry Risk
- Insulin has short expiry (6 months)
- Needs strict monitoring

### 4. Supplier Performance
- Supplier C has longest lead time (8–10 days)
- May impact critical drugs availability

---

## Recommendations

- Increase reorder frequency for high-demand drugs
- Reduce overstock of low-risk medications
- Improve supplier selection for critical items
- Implement safety stock strategy

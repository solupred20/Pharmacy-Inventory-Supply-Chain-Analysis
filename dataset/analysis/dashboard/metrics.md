# Supply Chain Metrics

## Key Metrics

### 1. Stock Coverage
Stock Level / Monthly Demand

### 2. Reorder Point
Demand × Lead Time

### 3. Safety Stock
Extra stock to prevent shortages

---

## Example

- Insulin:
  - Demand: 120
  - Lead Time: 10 days
  - High risk → needs safety stock

---

## Insights

- High lead time increases stockout risk
- Demand variability affects planning
